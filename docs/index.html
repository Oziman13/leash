Give your AI agent money, not your wallet.

Leash holds an agent's USDC budget on Arc. Payments inside your rules settle in seconds and are final the moment they land. Anything outside them waits for your tap. One switch freezes the agent completely.

Gas included. On Arc, gas is paid in USDC, so Leash refuels the agent's gas from the same dollar budget. "$1 a day" really means $1 a day, gas and all. On chains where gas is a separate volatile token, that promise is impossible.
